## 👀 At a Glance

PDF Editor for Windows is a free PDF text editor built for speed. It opens fast, loads your document straight away, and puts the editing tools you reach for most within a single click. When a deadline is close and a file just needs one quick fix, you should not have to wait on a heavy program or a slow online converter. This app is made for exactly those moments.

Speed does not mean it skips the essentials. You can rewrite text, swap images, add pages, annotate drafts, and complete forms, all in the same window. Everything runs locally on your desktop, so large files open quickly and your documents stay private. Make the change, export the result, and get on with your day.

1. Open PDF Editor and drag your document into the window.

3. Grab the markup tools to highlight or note anything for review.
4. Export the file and your updated PDF is ready to send.

## ❓ FAQ

**How fast can I make a quick edit?**
Very fast. Open the file, click into the text, type your change, and export, often in under a minute.

**Is it free to download and use?**
Yes. PDF Editor is a free PDF editor for Windows with no subscription required to edit your documents.

**Can I edit both text and images in the same file?**
You can. It works as a combined PDF text and image editor, so you handle both in one place.

**Do large documents slow it down?**
Not typically. Because it runs locally on your Windows desktop, even sizable files open and edit smoothly.
